An apology

About LiveLeak.site

We owe two apologies about LiveLeak.site. One for starting it, and one for how it ended.

We asked people to make accounts. We asked them to upload, to moderate each other, to show up every day and turn an empty site into something worth visiting. Then we shut it down and pointed everyone at SoboVids.com instead, and SoboVids.com has since shut down too. For the disruption, for the content people lost, and for making a community move house twice in one year: we are sorry.

That is the second apology. The first one is harder to write, so here it is in the founder's own words rather than ours.

“I have seen a lot of things in my life, but the minds that I let on my site… were horrific. I thought I would be able to make something good of the LiveLeak name, but I was mistaken. To all of you sick, twisted people out there, I hope you find peace. As for the community that LiveLeak had — the good one — thank you.”

— Founder, Sobolak Technologies

We took a name with a history and assumed we could steer what came with it. We could not.

What that changes

Mostly it changes the pace. We are taking longer before launching anything new, and we would rather ship late than ask another group of people to build a home on something we have not thought all the way through. Being down to one person enforces that whether we like it or not.

If you were part of the good side of that community: thank you, genuinely. You deserved a better ending than the one you got. There is one more thing the founder wanted to say to you.

Notice

SoboVids.com is discontinued. It is not accepting uploads, registrations, or support requests.
