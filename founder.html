A note from the founder

It is just me right now

The short version: Sobolak Technologies is not doing well. The team is gone, the founder is the only one left, and everything is moving slowly while we get back on our feet.

I would rather you read that here than work it out from the silence.

The team is gone. The people who built SoboAI, the people who kept the video platforms running, the people who answered the support mail — they have all moved on. I do not hold it against a single one of them. What is left of this company is one person in Michigan with the servers in the next room.

That is why everything is slow now. There is nobody to hand a task to. The restorations, the new AI work, the mail, this website — it is all one pair of hands. If a page looks stale or a reply takes a week, that is the reason, and it is not going to be fixed by pretending otherwise.

We are not closing

We will get back on our feet. The plan is small and specific: open the computer shop, get SoboAI 2.0 into a state worth showing anyone, and rebuild from there. Quieter than last time, and slower, but rebuilt. This company has started over before.

To everyone who was here

“I'm glad I had the community that I had while I had it. Sobolak Technologies wants to give all of you our biggest thanks. We love you all.”

— Founder, Sobolak Technologies

That is the whole message. Thank you for the years you spent on our sites, for the uploads, the arguments, the bug reports, and for sticking around through more than one ending.

Check back in a while and see what one person managed to do.

Who Is Left

One person. The founder handles the servers, the mail, the restorations, and this website.

Replies are slow. They do come.
