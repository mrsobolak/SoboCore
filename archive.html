Things we made that are gone

We would rather leave this list up than quietly delete it. If you used one of these, here is what happened to it.

SoboVids.com Retired 2026

Our flagship community video platform. Launched in 2026, shut down in late summer of the same year. It is not accepting uploads, registrations, or support requests, and it is not coming back. Rest in peace.

LiveLeak.site Retired 2026

A video platform we started and then closed, pushing its users toward SoboVids.com. We have more to say about this one.

SoboAI — Local AI Suite Retired Early 2026

A locally hosted AI assistant for Windows, with multi-model support and full offline capability. Discontinued in early 2026 when the company moved to media. A successor is in development.
