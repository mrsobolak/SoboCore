Who we are

Sobolak Technologies is an independent technology company in Michigan, United States, founded in 2024. One person, no investors, no outside money.

Today we refurbish and resell computers, build gaming PCs, and fix laptops. That work pays for the thing we actually want to make: the Sobolak Laptop, a handheld desktop built from recycled parts on our own motherboard, due around 2029.

What we believe

  • It is either ours or it is not ours. We design the board and make the case. Nothing rebadged.
  • Nothing is scrap until it fails a test. A ten-year-old desktop CPU still does what it did on day one. We put it back to work.
  • Big and heavy is fine. Power first. The battery is sized to match, not the other way round.
  • Screws, not glue. Ribbon cables, replaceable parts, and a machine you can open.
  • Say so. If something is an idea, we call it an idea. No fake launch dates.
