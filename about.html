About the company

Three chapters, two of them closed

Sobolak Technologies is an independent technology company based in Michigan, United States. It is privately held, it has taken no investment, and it answers to nobody but the people using its software.

First: local software

We started as a local-first software company. The idea was simple and we still believe in it — AI tools that run entirely on hardware you own, with no cloud account, no telemetry, and no dependency on a company staying in business. That was SoboAI, and it worked.

That chapter closed in early 2026 when the focus moved to media.

Second: video

We launched SoboVids.com as our flagship community video platform. Before it, briefly, there was LiveLeak.site. Both are gone. SoboVids.com shut down in late summer 2026, and there is a separate page about how the LiveLeak.site part of that went, because it deserves its own page rather than a paragraph.

Third: whatever this is

Right now we are between chapters, and smaller than we have ever been. The team is gone; the founder is running what is left on his own. Nothing we make is currently live. The founder's note covers that honestly.

The work has not stopped. AI development is back on the roadmap, and we are opening a storefront for computers — machines from the nineties through the twenties, restored and tested. Both are on Coming Soon.

What we care about

Not a mission statement. Just the handful of things we keep coming back to when there is a decision to make:

  • Own the hardware. If it runs on someone else's machine, it is on loan.
  • No censorship past what the law and plain ethics require — and we have learned the hard way where that line actually sits.
  • Community first, which is easy to say and is the thing we got wrong.
  • Built to last, whether that is a ninety-something tower or software that still opens in five years.
