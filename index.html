Welcome to Sobolak Technologies

We are a small independent technology company in Michigan, founded in 2024. Over three chapters we have built local AI software, run community video platforms, and shut both of those down.

Company update: things are not going well right now. The team is gone and the founder is the only one left. Read the note.

Where things stand

Nothing we make is live today. The staff has moved on, the products are in the archive, and the company is one person and a room full of servers in Michigan.

We are not closing. The plan is to get back on our feet: open the computer shop, get the new AI software into a state worth showing, and rebuild from there. It will take a while, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise.

What is being worked on

  • Computer sales — vintage and modern machines, the nineties through the twenties. Restored, tested, sold direct.
  • SoboAI 2.0 — a second attempt at the local AI software. Your machine, your models, your data.
  • SoboHost.site — cheap website hosting, on our own servers. More below, though not much more.

None of them has a launch date. We are not going to invent one to fill the space.

SoboHost.site

Cheap website hosting is coming soon. That is genuinely most of what we can say right now — the domain is registered, the servers are already on and doing nothing, and the math works out better than you would expect. ;)

We have had a room full of hardware in Michigan sitting idle since the video platforms went dark. Putting other people's websites on it is the obvious thing to do with it, so that is what SoboHost.site is going to be: small, plain, inexpensive hosting from a company that has to keep the lights on anyway.

No prices, no plans, no signup, and no date yet. When there is something to sign up for, it will show up here and on the Coming Soon page first.
