Why it costs less here

  • No marketplace cut.eBay charges sellers a fee on every sale, and sellers build that into the price. We sell direct, so it comes off yours.
  • No warehouse.One person, one workbench. There is not much overhead to pay for.

Before a machine gets sold

Four things happen to every computer that leaves the bench.

  1. TestedProcessor, memory, drive, screen, keyboard, ports, battery. If something fails, it gets fixed or the machine does not get sold.
  2. Opened and cleanedDust out, case wiped down. Screws, not glue.
  3. Wiped and reinstalledThe last owner's data is erased and Windows goes back on fresh.
  4. Graded honestlyYou hear about the wear before you pay. If it is a scuff, we call it a scuff.
  • Grade ALight wear. You have to look for it.
  • Grade BVisible wear. Works exactly the same.
  • Grade CRough outside. Cheap, and it runs.
