The Sobolak Laptop (coming 2029~)


Desktop power at your fingertips. Recycled, saving the planet.

The Sobolak Laptop is a handheld desktop. It is thick and heavy on purpose. Inside is a full-power desktop processor and memory, pulled from recycled desktop computers and put onto a motherboard we design ourselves, in a case we make ourselves. Expect about three to four hours on the battery, because it runs real desktop chips.

It is either ours or it is not ours. No rebadged boards, no bought-in modules. It boots to a Sobolak screen, ships with Windows 10, and you can put whatever you like on it after that.

Where it stands: it is an idea we are serious about. The board design has not started. We are aiming for 2029, give or take, and we will not put a fake date on it to look busy.
