About Sobolak Technologies

An independent technology company in Michigan, United States. Founded 2024. Privately held, no investors, one person on staff.

We have started over before. This time the work is hardware: computers built from parts the rest of the industry has written off. Earlier projects are retired and not coming back, and the pages about them have come down. This site is about what comes next.

What we care about

  • Own the hardware. If it runs on someone else's machine, it is on loan.
  • Nothing is scrap until it fails a test. A ten-year-old CPU still does what it did on day one.
  • Built to last. Repairable, documented, and boring in the good way.
  • Say so. If something is an idea, we call it an idea. No fake launch dates.

Chipset recycling

Every dead laptop has a CPU, memory and a chipset that still work. The board they are soldered to is what died. We are designing a board they can live on instead.

How it is meant to work

  1. Old laptops come in. We pull the processor, memory and supporting chips and test them.
  2. Working parts go onto the Sobolak motherboard, a design of ours that accepts several generations of chipsets rather than one.
  3. The board goes into the handheld desktop: a small box with a screen, a keyboard port, and a desktop operating system. Not a phone. A computer you can hold.
  4. The rest is recycled properly, not landfilled.

Status of the program

Not open. We are not taking machines yet, and we will not ask anyone to ship hardware to us until there is a board to put it on. When the program opens, it opens here first.

Note This is a design-stage project. The motherboard is a schematic, not a product.
