We build software, and lately we take things apart.

Sobolak Technologies is a small independent company in Michigan. We are between chapters right now, and we would rather say that plainly than dress it up.

The short version: we started in 2024 writing local-first AI software that ran on your own machine. In early 2026 we put that down and moved to video, which ended the way it ended. Nothing of ours is live today.

Two things are being worked on — a computer shop selling machines from the nineties through the twenties, and a second attempt at the AI software. Neither has a date, and we are not going to invent one.
